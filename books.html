Helpful Information

Books FAQ

Can I request book titles not listed on your website?

Yes, absolutely! We are a boutique physical bookstore, so our website highlights curation themes rather than an extensive database. You can request any book (in-print or rare/out-of-print) through our Special Order Request Form on the Contact page.

Do you carry books in languages other than English?

We keep a dedicated shelf of imported French, Spanish, Portuguese, and German literature in their original translations. If there is a specific edition you would like, our special order desk can import it for you.

Can I host my local book club meeting in your reading lounge?

Yes! We love hosting local reading groups. Space reservations are free, but we request that you contact us in advance to check availability and reserve our large oak discussion table.

Looking for a Hard-to-Find Title?

Submit a request to our Special Order Desk and let us track down rare or out-of-print books.
