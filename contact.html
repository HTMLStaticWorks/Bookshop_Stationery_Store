Common Questions

Contact & Orders FAQ

How much does it cost to request a special order?

Submitting an order request is completely free. We will search our systems and respond with the price of the book and estimated shipping. We only request payment once you confirm you want to proceed with the order.

How long do special orders take to arrive?

Standard domestic book orders usually arrive in 3-5 business days. Rare collector printings or international translations imported from Europe or Asia can take between 10-21 days depending on distributor locations.

Can I pick up my books at your storefront?

Yes! We offer free storefront pickup. We will notify you via email as soon as your items have been shelved at our checkout desk.

What is your return policy for book orders?

In-store catalog books can be returned within 14 days in their original unread condition. Special imports and rare out-of-print books cannot be returned or cancelled once the shipping process has started.

Looking for a Hard-to-Find Title?

Submit a request to our Special Order Desk and let us track down rare or out-of-print books.
