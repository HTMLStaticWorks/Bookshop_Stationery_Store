Preserving Print Culture

Our Story

Founded in 2020 by former literature professors and bookbinders, Bookshop started with a mission to keep physical reading culture alive in the digital age. We noticed a lack of community spaces where readers could gather, discuss, and enjoy fine tactile stationery, and set out to bridge the gap.

Today, we have grown into a premier literary sanctuary featuring over 1,500 curated titles, a cozy wood-paneled reading lounge, and weekly creative writing workshops. We continue to support independent publishers and offer hand-pressed customized bindings for collectors.

The Core Goal

Our Mission

Our mission is to foster intellectual curiosity and creative expression by providing human-curated literature and artisan writing tools. We believe that physical books and handwritten correspondence connect people in ways that digital mediums cannot replicate.

By prioritizing quality materials, independent authors, and welcoming spaces, we aim to preserve and support the literary arts in our local community.
